Our Story

Built to make finding a home in Nigeria simple again

ACCOOM is a marketplace for finding and listing premium properties. We connect active, capable buyers and renters with trusted, verified agents on one secure, transparent platform, so every search ends with a place worth moving into.

From single rooms to luxury apartments, every listing on ACCOOM is tied to a real agent profile with a verification level, a rating and a response history, so you always know exactly who you're dealing with before you reach out.
