Check an ACTD

Enter the ACTD number printed on the document, or scan its QR code.

Important. ACCOOM does not have a server yet. This check can only look at records stored in this browser, so a document issued on another device will show as “not found” here even if it is genuine. “Not found” does not mean a document is fake. Public checking from any device needs a backend.

This page never shows names, amounts, addresses or contact details.
